# Idea Lens

Use only when the user's goal includes research-idea exploration or when a material finding justifies it.

Build internally:

`observation -> Candidate Gap -> research question -> hypothesis -> minimal experiment -> possible contribution -> risks`

Keep the result as a Candidate Idea until the internal gap has enough support and any novelty/feasibility checks required by the intended claim have been performed. Never infer field-level novelty from one paper.

In ordinary user-facing answers, present the idea and its maturity in natural language. Do not expose raw gap/novelty field names merely because they are tracked internally; show structured status only when requested or when audit/export/precise tracking benefits from it.
