# Relevance Lens

Use when the user asks whether a paper relates to a current problem, knowledge need, or research direction.

- State the goal being evaluated.
- Judge relevance to that goal, not vague overall paper value.
- Identify the paper parts that create the relevance.
- Do not equate topical similarity or high relevance with a Research Gap, novelty, or a recommended research direction.
- Stop once the relevance decision and next reading target are adequately supported.
